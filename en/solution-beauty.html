One device, three analyses

Skin analysis

7 items

The dedicated device captures seven items. Item-specific algorithms and AI models, built on data gathered with partner institutions, rate each one on a scale of 1 to 5. Results lead straight to personalized solutions and product recommendations.

What we analyze
Checks moisture and elasticity to find skin that needs hydration before the barrier weakens.
Uses T-zone and U-zone oil levels to classify skin as dry, normal, oily or combination.
UV imaging reveals sebum inside pores, so shine and breakouts can be anticipated.
Finds and marks pores, grades how enlarged they are, and flags skin that needs pore care.
Maps melanin distribution to flag skin that needs brightening and UV protection.
Grades everything from fine texture lines to deep eye wrinkles and shows when to start firming care.
Outlines areas reacting to irritation to identify customers who need gentle, soothing products.

Skin tone analysis

4 tones

Measures the skin color of the forehead and both cheeks as RGB values and classifies it into four tones by brightness and warm or cool undertone. It shows matching colors and recommends the color cosmetics registered for each tone.

What we analyze
A light, cool tone. Suits rose lips, purple or blue eyeshadow and soft black or dark brown hair.
A calm, deep tone. Suits gray or purple eyeshadow and black or dark brown hair.
A warm, radiant tone. Suits coral or orange lips, light brown or yellow beige eyeshadow and light brown hair.
A deep, warm tone. Suits olive, bronze or gold brown eyeshadow and dark brown or mahogany hair.

Scalp & hair analysis

Scalp + hair loss

A single scalp capture analyzes hair density, hair thickness, scalp type and sensitivity at once. For hair loss, a smartphone photo of the crown classifies the pattern and stage. Results lead straight to personalized solutions and product recommendations.

What we analyze
Classifies the scalp as dry, normal or oily, a basis for choosing shampoo and care products that fit sebum output.
Counts hairs per cm² and shows the result as low, normal or high.
Measures hair diameter (mm) and sorts hair into fine, normal or thick, a basis for volume and nourishing care.
Rates the scalp as normal or sensitive to flag scalps that need gentle, soothing care.
A crown photo identifies the hair loss pattern (M-shaped, crown, part line and more) and grades its progress on a 7-stage scale for men and a 4-stage scale for women.
RECOMMEND

This is the only consultation tool you need.
Just register your products.

Skin, skin tone and scalp results are quantified as stages and types for each item. Register your brand's products with tags, and the recommendation algorithm picks the ones that match each result and shows them with purchase links.

01

Register products

Enter product details and choose tags that link to results, such as moisture, pores, hair loss or Cool Bright. You can also upload a whole catalog from Excel.

02

Capture · Quantify

When a manager captures a customer, the app returns a 1 to 5 rating for each item, skin type, personal tone, scalp type and hair loss stage. Scores come with interpretations, giving the manager's consultation a solid basis.

03

Recommend · Purchase

Products are recommended for the items that need care most. Low moisture brings up hydrating products, deep wrinkles bring up anti-wrinkle products, and progressing hair loss brings up hair loss products.
