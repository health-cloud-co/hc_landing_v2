GMES 2026: Bellus skin and scalp analysis and nailfold capillary analysis demonstrated

We took part in the 22nd Gangwon Medical Equipment Show (GMES 2026) on 3–4 September 2026, demonstrating the Bellus skin, scalp and hair analysis service and the nailfold capillary (NFC) condition analysis service on site.

During the show we met 13 parties, including overseas buyers from Bangladesh, South Africa, India, Indonesia, Sri Lanka, Nigeria, the United States, Hong Kong and Australia as well as Korean companies and universities, and follow-up discussions are under way on sample and documentation requests.
