Wellness Through Capillaries

Capillary observation used to depend on hospital microscopes. A portable device and on-device AI bring it into everyday wellness.
Five indices scored from fingertip capillaries are combined with a lifestyle questionnaire to provide a wellness type and coaching.

WHY CAPILLARY

Capillaries are a leading indicator

App screen 2
App screen 3

Blood pressure and pulse often change only after someone collapses. They are lagging indicators that move after the event. Peripheral capillaries are a leading indicator that signals first. The nailfold is the only place where these capillaries can be seen as they are without cutting the skin, which is why it is called the body's living window. The effects of supplements and lifestyle changes also show up here first.

Directly observes the microcirculation that makes up 80 to 95 percent of the body's vascular network
Body composition takes at least 3 months to show results. Capillaries take 4 to 8 weeks
How it works
  1. 01Capture · AnalyzeApply oil to the nail, place it on the device and focus. The 200x optical system images the nailfold capillaries non-invasively. Each capillary loop is located, its thickness and shape are measured in micrometers, and the results are scored across five indices.
  2. 02Lifestyle questionnaireA survey records six areas (diet, physical activity, sleep, mental health, alcohol and smoking) and scores them out of 100, with scores and improvement guides for each area.
  3. 03Type · CoachingCrossing the capillary score with the lifestyle score places each person in one of nine wellness types, with coaching for each type. As regular measurements accumulate, the time-series trajectory is analyzed to suggest solutions matched to how each person responds.

Result · Cause · Solution
Three indices

The result read from the vessels (NWI) is crossed with the cause found in lifestyle (LWI) to reach a personalized solution (NMI).

THE WHAT · Result
NWI

Nailfold Wellness Index

AI models trained on data gathered with partner institutions, together with our own algorithms, quantify five indices. The five are combined into a score out of 100.

  • DensityCapillary loops per mm
  • ThicknessAverage outer diameter at the loop apex
  • ShapeShare of U-shaped loops
  • DamageNumber of microhemorrhages
  • LossAvascular areas wider than 500 µm
THE WHY · Cause
LWI

Lifestyle Wellness Index

Six areas are measured by survey and normalized to 100. Psychosocial items are added for people 65 and older. Even with good vascular indices, a low lifestyle score triggers preventive guidance.

THE SO WHAT · Solution
NMI

Nailfold Matrix Index NFC Matrix Index

NWI and LWI are crossed on a two-dimensional grid to show, as probabilities, where a person sits among nine wellness types. Each measurement adds a point, and the trajectory tracks recovery or decline. The effects of care appear on the trajectory within 4 to 8 weeks.

  • TypeNine types combining high, middle and low vascular and lifestyle scores
  • CoachingA one-line briefing on strengths, indices to improve and the habits behind them
  • GuideNutrition, diet and exercise guides for each type
  • AlertWhen a sharp decline is detected, an expert consultation is recommended instead of coaching

What three months of tracking showed

We ran two three-month tracking pilots with Company A, a global health supplement company. In the second pilot, 15 participants took supplements and were measured four times at four-week intervals. Vessel wall function recovered first, by week 4. Shape regularity and vessel density then improved in turn, and the overall index began rising at week 8 and gained 14 points on average by week 12.

  • Weeks 0 to 4Function recoversThickness stability · Vessel integrity
  • Weeks 4 to 8Structure realignsShape regularity
  • Weeks 8 to 12Density increasesVessel density · Uniform arrangement

COACHING

Coaching matched to each result

Diet, nutrition and exercise guides change with the nine wellness types and index scores. We follow a minimal supplementation principle, adding only the one or two nutrients food does not cover, and link to supplement brands' catalogs so the guides can be used right in consultations.

01NutritionRecommends only the one or two nutrients that are lacking for each type and index, including nutrients depleted by prescription drugs for chronic conditions.
02DietStarts with a standard diet free of processed food. Guides a fiber-first eating order (vegetables, then protein, then carbohydrates) and water intake matched to body weight.
03Exercise · Daily lifeGuides nutrient timing around workouts and low to moderate intensity cardio, and works on sleep and stress where lifestyle scores are low.
